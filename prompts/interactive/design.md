# Design an engineering solution

## Inputs
- Problem and goals: [context]
- Functional and nonfunctional requirements: [constraints]
- Existing architecture: [interfaces and boundaries]
- Explicit non-goals: [exclusions]

## Workflow
1. Clarify requirements and identify invariants.
2. Propose at least two viable alternatives when meaningful.
3. Compare complexity, reliability, security, performance, cost, and migration impact.
4. Recommend one option with risks and assumptions.
5. Define interfaces, rollout strategy, test strategy, and measurable acceptance criteria.

Avoid invented benchmarks or claims unsupported by evidence.
